# OPC Launch IP Check

Map each product asset to the protection, evidence, agreement, or official requirement that may apply before launch. Do not imply that one registration protects everything. This matters because assets can have different owners, evidence chains, filing paths, and disclosure tradeoffs.

## Source and legal boundary

Adapt this workflow from the one-person-company, asset-rights, trademark, domain, software copyright, patent, trade-secret, contract, and launch-compliance themes in [小山学堂 · AI 从入门到精通](https://xueai.app/slides/home.html), created by 洛小山 / 米羊科技. This adaptation was prepared on 2026-08-11. Keep the bundled AGPL-3.0 license and attribution when redistributing it.

Begin every deliverable with:

> 本结果仅用于风险识别与材料准备，不构成正式法律意见，也不保证权利可注册、可执行或产品已经合规。

Do not file, pay, purchase, contact a third party, submit material, accept terms, or launch anything without explicit action-time authorization.

## Collect the seven inputs

Collect or mark missing:

1. **产品名** — Chinese and foreign-language names, logo or mark, product category, and intended use.
2. **域名** — candidate or registered domains, registrant, administrative account, and renewal control.
3. **代码和内容来源** — founder, employee, contractor, open source, purchased, licensed, commissioned, or AI-generated; include datasets, prompts, images, audio, video, and dependencies when relevant.
4. **合作方** — contributor, contractor, cofounder, supplier, platform, or distributor; record deliverables, contract, confidentiality, and rights-assignment status.
5. **是否有核心算法或配方** — include algorithms, recipes, data processes, prompts, operational methods, and non-public know-how.
6. **上线地区** — allow multiple jurisdictions and channels; never mix rules from different jurisdictions.
7. **公司主体状态** — not formed, forming, or active; identify mismatches between the intended entity and the holder of domains, contracts, repositories, stores, payment accounts, or filings.

## Verify current official sources

Before making a jurisdiction-specific legal, filing, fee, or platform claim:

1. Open the relevant official government, registry, court, regulator, or platform source.
2. Record jurisdiction, official institution, direct `source_url`, applicable or effective date, and `verified_at` as ISO 8601 with timezone for this check.
3. Distinguish the source text from the inference about this product.
4. Mark conflicts, account-specific rules, and professional questions.

Search summaries, blogs, agencies, and course material can help locate an issue but cannot substitute for the current official source. If the official source cannot be verified, label the conclusion `法规结论待核验` and prepare questions or materials instead of stating a definitive rule.

## Check all six locks

Review every lock as `已覆盖`, `缺失`, `证据不足`, `待官方核验`, or `不适用`. Verify its legal scope in the launch jurisdiction; the descriptions below are issue-routing labels, not jurisdiction-independent legal conclusions.

1. **商标** — route product names, logos, and source-identifying marks to clearance, class or service analysis, applicant ownership, and filing evidence.
2. **域名** — route the product's digital entry point to registration, registrant identity, account control, renewal, and dispute exposure. Do not treat domain control as a substitute for trademark rights.
3. **软件著作权** — route software source and ownership evidence to the jurisdiction's software-copyright or registration mechanism where one exists.
4. **著作权** — route original text, images, audio, video, interface expression, documentation, and other content to authorship, ownership, license, and evidence questions.
5. **专利** — route a potentially protectable technical solution to novelty, disclosure timing, inventorship, ownership, business value, cost, and professional patentability review.
6. **商业秘密** — route valuable non-public algorithms, recipes, prompts, data processes, and operating know-how to secrecy, access control, confidentiality, evidence, and incident response.

Contracts and entity records support the ownership chain across these locks; they do not replace the six-lock analysis.

When performing a full OPC asset and IP check, read `references/ip-checklist.md` before building the six-lock matrix and material list. Skip it only for one narrow terminology question.

## Prioritize timing

Use:

- `必须现在做` for name clearance, ownership gaps, planned public disclosure, missing consent or license, required launch permission, entity mismatch, or another potentially irreversible loss;
- `可以延后` only when delay preserves the option and does not block the intended launch;
- `必须专业复核` when a jurisdiction-specific conclusion, registrability, patentability, contract interpretation, data or consumer obligation, dispute, or filing strategy exceeds issue spotting.

## Return this structure

1. **风险识别与材料准备，非正式法律意见** — include the required disclaimer.
2. **极简结论** — `可继续`, `有条件继续`, or `先暂停关键动作` with the biggest reason.
3. **已知输入、缺失输入和关键假设**.
4. **六把锁矩阵** — for every lock show what it may protect, matching asset, current evidence, missing lock or evidence, priority, materials to prepare, and professional-review need.
5. **必须现在做**.
6. **可以延后** — include the condition or trigger for revisiting it.
7. **必须请专业人士复核** — question, professional type, and materials to bring.
8. **官方来源核验记录** — jurisdiction, conclusion, institution, direct `source_url`, applicable/effective date, and `verified_at`.
9. **最小下一步材料清单** — preparation only unless further action is explicitly authorized.

Do not turn this into a general startup or product-launch strategy report. Keep ownership, IP, source evidence, agreements, and applicable launch obligations in scope.
